Creamy Fettuccine Alfredo

A luxurious Roman classic made with bronze-cut fettuccine ribbons coated in an emulsion of 24-month aged Parmigiano-Reggiano, sweet European butter, and cracked telicherry black pepper.

Prep Time ⏱ 10 mins
Cook Time 🍳 15 mins
Total Time ⏳ 25 mins
Servings 🍽 2 Servings
Difficulty 🟡 Medium
👨‍🍳 Preserved through Git resolution • Modify/Delete conflict resolved

Ingredients (Check as you cook)

Generous portions for two guests.

💡 Chef's Secret Ingredient

Never discard your pasta water! The suspended starches are the emulsifier that bonds cream and cheese together.

Cooking Procedure

1

Boil Pasta Al Dente

Bring a large pot of salted water (1 tablespoon kosher salt per 4 quarts water) to a rolling boil. Cook fettuccine until 1 minute shy of package instructions (firm al dente). Scoop out and reserve ½ cup of starchy pasta water, then drain pasta.

⏱ 8–10 minutes
2

Melt Butter & Bloom Garlic

In a wide deep sauté pan over medium-low heat, gently melt the unsalted butter. Add the microplaned garlic and swirl for 45 seconds until fragrant but completely free of color.

⏱ 1 minute
3

Simmer Cream & Warm Spices

Pour in the heavy whipping cream. Bring to a gentle simmer for 3 minutes, letting the cream slightly reduce and thicken. Whisk in cracked black pepper and a micro-pinch of nutmeg.

⏱ 3 minutes
4

Emulsify the Parmigiano & Pasta

Remove pan from direct heat. Toss in the drained fettuccine ribbons and gradually sprinkle in the grated Parmigiano-Reggiano while vigorously tossing with tongs. Splash in 2 to 3 tablespoons of hot reserved pasta water to create a glossy, clingy sauce.

⏱ 2 minutes

Wine Pairing & Serving

Plate immediately in warm shallow pasta bowls. Garnish with an extra dusting of aged parmesan, fresh cracked black pepper, and torn Italian basil. Pair with a crisp, acidic Pinot Grigio or dry Italian Chianti.
