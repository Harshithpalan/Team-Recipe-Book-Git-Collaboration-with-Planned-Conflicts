Golden Buttermilk Pancakes

Cloud-soft on the inside with delicate crisp edges, these buttermilk pancakes are an absolute weekend staple. Accented with pure Madagascar vanilla and studded with fresh wild blueberries.

Prep Time ⏱ 10 mins
Cook Time 🍳 10 mins
Total Time ⏳ 20 mins
Servings 🍽 4 Servings
Difficulty 🟢 Easy
👨‍🍳 Developed by Developer A • Branch: feature/breakfast-recipes

Ingredients (Check as you prep)

Yields approximately 8 large, fluffy pancakes.

💡 Equipment Needed

Cast iron griddle or heavy non-stick skillet, wire whisk, and a silicone spatula.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Whisk the Dry Ingredients

In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, granulated sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and sea salt until thoroughly distributed and aerated.

⏱ 2 minutes
2

Emulsify the Wet Batter

In a separate medium bowl or measuring jug, vigorously whisk the buttermilk, eggs, melted butter, and vanilla extract until smooth and frothy.

⏱ 2 minutes
3

Combine with Care (The Golden Rule)

Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients. Fold gently with a spatula just until combined. Crucial rule: Leave small lumps! Over-mixing develops gluten and turns pancakes chewy instead of airy. Let the batter rest on the counter for 5 minutes.

⏱ 5 minutes rest
4

Griddle to Golden Perfection

Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-low heat. Brush lightly with butter. Ladle ⅓ cup of batter per pancake. Cook until surface bubbles pop and stay open, about 2½ to 3 minutes. Flip gently and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes until golden brown.

⏱ 5 minutes per batch

👨‍🍳 Developer A's Kitchen Secret

Never press down on pancakes with your spatula after flipping! Pressing forces out trapped steam pockets, causing them to deflate. For maximum fluffiness, let the residual heat gently puff them to height.

Serving Suggestions & Pairings

Stack high with salted French butter, Grade-A dark amber maple syrup, and a sprinkle of powdered sugar. Pairs sublimely with freshly brewed Colombian dark roast coffee or cold-pressed Valencia orange juice.
