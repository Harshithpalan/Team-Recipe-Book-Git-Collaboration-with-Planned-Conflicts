Rainbow Citrus Fruit Salad

A dazzling celebration of color, crunch, and natural sweetness. Jewel-toned organic berries, sliced kiwi coins, and golden Ataulfo mango cubes dressed in a zesty citrus-mint reduction.

Prep Time ⏱ 15 mins
Cook Time ❄ 0 mins
Total Time ⏳ 15 mins
Servings 🍽 4 Servings
Difficulty 🟢 Very Easy
👨‍🍳 Developed jointly by Dev A & Dev B • Shared Baseline Recipe

Ingredients (Check as you prep)

Serves 4 refreshing bowls.

💡 Seasonality Tip

Feel free to substitute seasonal stone fruits like white peaches, nectarines, or dark sweet cherries in the summer.

Preparation Steps

1

Wash and Dry Thoroughly

Rinse all berries gently under cold running water. Spread them over paper towels to dry completely. Water left on fresh fruit dilutes the dressing and prevents it from clinging properly.

⏱ 5 minutes
2

Chop and Arrange the Fruits

Slice strawberries into quarters, slice the kiwis into half-moons, and dice the mango into clean bite-sized cubes. Transfer all prepared fruit into a wide glass or ceramic serving bowl.

⏱ 5 minutes
3

Whisk the Citrus Mint Dressing

In a small ramekin or jar, whisk together the fresh lime juice, wild honey, and chopped fresh mint until the honey is completely dissolved and emulsion is smooth.

⏱ 2 minutes
4

Dress, Chill, and Serve

Drizzle the honey-lime dressing evenly over the fruit right before serving. Toss very gently with two large wooden spoons to avoid bruising the tender berries. Chill for 10 minutes in the refrigerator before presenting.

⏱ 3 minutes

🍓 Team Kitchen Note

The lime juice serves a dual purpose: its bright acidity balances the rich sweetness of mango and honey, while the citric acid prevents sliced apples, pears, and bananas from oxidizing and turning brown.
